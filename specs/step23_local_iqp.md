# Step 23 spec: a device-connectivity prior makes the IQP identifiable — local IQP vs MaxEnt on grid data

Status: **frozen** once committed. Written 2026-11-13 after the step 23 exploration (notes/2026-11-13_step23_sparse_iqp_exploration.md
and branch `step18-adversarial` `explore/step23/`). Claude chose this step under the user's standing instruction to take
the recommended option. Every number in §4 is fixed here, before any model is fitted on the test seeds 920–933 or 940–953.

**Acceptance:** `python -m src.step23.run --verify` must exit 0. It is the definition of done; a FAIL hypothesis is a
result, not a reason to change the check.

## 1. Why (the theory this step tests)

Exploration on spent seeds found:
- **Non-identifiability.** For the generic IQP (696 weight-≤3 generators) fitted to order-≤3 moments, the low-order
  Jacobian has condition number 5e8–1.6e10. Its weakest 100 directions move orders 4–6 45–334× more than orders 1–3, while
  the strongest 100 move them 2.2–2.8×. The delta-method prediction of G(N) is therefore exact for MaxEnt (0.91–1.07× the
  measured value) and useless for the IQP (10²–10¹³×). The IQP's completion is decided by where the optimiser stops,
  not by the data.
  - Evidence: ridge penalties λ ∈ {1e-6 … 1e-3} change R by ±0.15 with no direction.
- **A real structural prior fixes it.** With the support known (oracle), a sparse IQP reaches R ≈ 0.2–0.34 at N = 1e4.
  - The non-oracle version of that prior is the **device connectivity graph**: on hardware, which qubits are coupled is
    known in advance; which couplings are active and their angles are not.
  - Pilot (dev seeds 900–903, 4×4 grid), 85 grid-local candidates, 20 restarts, init std 0.3:
    - R vs MaxEnt(3) is 0.04, 0.29, 0.17, 0.50 at N = ∞ and 0.37, 0.15, 0.68, 0.47 at N = 1e4;
    - R vs MaxEnt given the same graph is 0.003–0.14.
- **Mechanism for the classical side.** The generators are local, but interference spreads correlations off the graph in
  log-probability space. MaxEnt restricted to the graph is therefore 3–50× worse than full MaxEnt(3): the classical
  model cannot use the same prior.

**Hypothesis.** On coherent data generated by local couplings on a known graph, an IQP restricted to that graph is
identifiable from order-≤3 moments. It then beats both the best generic classical completion (MaxEnt(3)) and the
classical model given the same graph, at finite N.

**What this is not.** It is not a computational quantum advantage. n = 16 is classically simulable, and the IQP's
moments are classically estimable at any n. This is a statement about the right inductive bias for coherent device data.

## 2. Data and models (load-bearing details)

- **Device graph.** A 4×4 grid, qubit q(r, c) = 4r + c. The candidate (local) generators are:
  - the 16 singles;
  - the 24 nearest-neighbour edges;
  - the 36 corner triples of the 9 plaquettes;
  - the 9 plaquettes (weight 4).

  That is 85 masks.
- **Target `grid_iqp(seed)`.**
  - IQP on singles + edges + plaquettes, 49 generators. The triples are absent: the model does not know which candidates
    are active.
  - θ = σ · N(0, 1) from `default_rng(seed)`, σ = step 14's SIGMA (0.251).
  - p is computed exactly by `iqp_distribution`.
- **Out-of-family safety target `grid_ising(seed)`** (report only):
  - p ∝ exp(Σ_i h_i s_i + Σ_edges J_ij s_i s_j), s = ±1;
  - h ~ 0.2·N(0,1) and J ~ 0.3·N(0,1) (guesses), from `default_rng(10_000 + seed)`.
  - This is a local classical log-linear model: MaxEnt-on-graph is its true family.
- **Training moments.** `training_moments(p, N, seed, 16)` (step 4): order-≤3 parities of N samples drawn with seed = seed.
- **Score.** G = `metrics.completion_gap(q, p, 16)["G"]`: orders 4–6, exact against p. There is no test noise.
- **Models.** All are fitted to the same 696 order-≤3 moments.
  - `iqp_local`: IQPModel with gens = the 85 local candidates; L-BFGS-B as in step 1; init θ ~ N(0, 0.3²);
    **20 restarts**; keep the lowest training residual. Init std 0.3 and 20 restarts were chosen on the dev seeds; both
    are guesses.
  - `me3`: MaxEnt(3), 696 parameters, unchanged.
  - `me_local`: MaxEnt on the 76 local masks of weight ≤ 3 (singles, edges, triples). This is the **fairness arm**: the
    classical model gets the same graph prior.
  - `iqp_generic`: IQPModel default (696 generators, init std 0.05, 3 restarts). Fitted only at N = 1e4, for the contrast
    hypothesis H4.
- Do not give any model the target's θ, its active set, or N = ∞ moments when N is finite. Any of these is oracle
  information.

R_x = G_iqp_local / G_x.

| ID | Statement | PASS if | Sign-test p under H0 (p = ½) |

Reported, with no verdict:
- median R for every arm;
- N = ∞ diagnostics;
- the training residual of iqp_local against the noise floor Σ(1 − f̂²)/N;
- the safety set: counts of R_me3 < 1 and R_me_local < 1 on grid_ising. The expected outcome is that the local IQP
  loses to me_local there. If it does, that is the correct behaviour of a wrong prior, not a failure.

## 5. Readings

- **A1, H1–H4 all PASS.** The finite-N loss of the generic IQP is a non-identifiability problem. A real device prior
  removes it. The resulting advantage cannot be copied by giving the classical model the same prior. This is the paper's
  positive result.
- **A2, H2 passes, H1 fails.** The graph helps the IQP far more than it helps a classical model, but not enough to beat
  unrestricted MaxEnt(3). Report as a partial result.
- **A3, H1 passes, H2 fails.** The win comes from the graph, not from the IQP. No IQP claim.
- **A4, H4 fails.** Restriction does not help. The exploration finding does not generalise.

## 6. Budget and stop-loss

- Pilot timing: one iqp_local job with 20 restarts takes about 18–25 min with 8 jobs in parallel. iqp_generic takes
  about 20 min.
- The total is 14 × (1e4, 1e5, ∞) + 14 safety = 56 local jobs and 14 generic jobs: about 3 h with 8 workers.
- **Stop-loss: 5 h wall clock** for the whole run. Jobs still running at 5 h are killed and reported as missing. A
  hypothesis with missing seeds counts its missing seeds as failures. Rerunning them needs a written amendment.
- Each job writes `results/step23/<set>/N_<N>/seed_<s>/metrics.json`, so the run is resumable.
